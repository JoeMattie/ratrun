## What Was Built

### 1. Drake Pixel Art — `src/game/art_duck.rs`
- Public contract implemented verbatim:
  - `DuckPose { Waddle, Tell(f32), Peck(f32), Recover(f32) }`
  - `DuckView { pos, facing, pose, walk, on_water, hurt, seed }`
  - `pub const PECK_REACH: f32 = 22.0`
  - `pub fn draw_duck(pb: &mut PixelBuffer, v: &DuckView, cam: Vec2)`
  - `pub fn bill_tip(v: &DuckView) -> Vec2`
- **Single source of truth for bill geometry**: `geometry(&DuckView)` computes all local `(u, v)` coordinates once; both `bill_tip` and `draw_duck` read `bill_tip_u` / `bill_v`, so hit tests, hostile-layer tell outlines, and rendered orange bill pixels agree to sub-pixel precision.
- **Silhouette & scale**: Big deadpan white domestic Pekin duck (~20 px long tail-to-bill, ~14 px wide with 1-px dark outline), plump oval body with curved cooler-grey folded-wing creases, short pointed tail, visible neck waist, round head with two dark side eyes, flat 4-px orange bill with subtle nail/nostril shading, and alternating orange webbed feet on dry floor. Cast drop shadow and water ripples stamp with material pen `0` so lighting specular/rim effects stay on the body.
- **Poses**:
  - `Waddle`: ~3 Hz lateral body rock (`±1 px`), stabilized head, alternating webbed feet stride; `bill_tip` stays at ~9.6 px (< 10 px).
  - `Tell(p)`: smoothstep coil-back; body squats wider and shifts back, rounded wings lift slightly at the flanks (no sharp wing tips), head pulls back and up over the chest with a collar shadow crease.
  - `Peck(p)`: smoothstep lunge out to exact `PECK_REACH` (`22.0 px`) at `p = 0.55` and smooth retraction by `p = 1.0`; tapering white neck segment extends while the round head keeps its size (`r = 2.45 px`).
  - `Recover(p)`: head lowered and shaking side to side (`±1.9 px` damped at 4.5 Hz), 3 deterministic loose ruffled feather pixels drifting off the flanks.
- **Modifiers**:
  - `on_water`: hides webbed feet, trims lower flank slightly, replaces ground shadow with a 1-px lighter pale-teal waterline ripple arc and trailing wake ring.
  - `hurt`: standard warm-white hit flash matching `art_enemy.rs` / `art_roster_sewer.rs`, with warm-tan rim tint (`(176, 150, 112)`) so the white duck retains its silhouette while flashing.

### 2. Drake Procedural Audio — `src/audio.rs`
- Appended `Sfx::Quack` and `Sfx::Peck` to `Sfx`, `Sfx::ALL` (`167 -> 169`), `Sfx::name` (`"QUACK"`, `"PECK"`), `Sfx::variant_count` (`3` each), `Sfx::priority` (`Quack => 1`, `Peck => 0`), `Sfx::min_spacing_ms` (`Peck => 60 ms`), and `sfx_variant_samples`.
- `Sfx::Quack` (140 ms, 3 variants, peak `0.30`): falling saw (`420 -> 295 Hz`) through a narrow nasal formant band-pass (`1520 -> 1240 Hz`, `Q = 3.6`) with 54 Hz amplitude wobble + lower bill/throat pulse formant (`840 -> 630 Hz`) + short reedy pink-noise chiff.
- `Sfx::Peck` (50 ms, 3 variants, peak `0.28`, rate-limited at 60 ms): crisp band-passed white-noise click (`2400 -> 1150 Hz`) + woody ~900 Hz triangle ping decaying over ~30 ms + light low woody knock body.

## Design Decisions
- Used rounded lifted wing pads during `Tell` (after v1's tapered wing shapes read too angular/fin-like in the visual preview) and added a visible neck waist between the plump oval body and round head at `Waddle`.
- Used a warm-tan hit-flash rim (`(176, 150, 112)`) rather than bright cream so the white duck keeps its outline contrast while flashing.
- Set `Sfx::Quack` priority to `1` (enemy attack tell, matching `Sfx::HornetBuzz`), `Sfx::Peck` priority to `0`, and `Sfx::Peck::min_spacing_ms` to `60 ms` so overlapping volleys from two Drakes cannot stack while never dropping a single Drake's 220 ms peck cadence.
