## Decisions (no approval needed, listed for review)

1. **`is_registration()` false for the guest.** Alternative was `true`, which would have broken two semantic tests (`every_registration_names_an_existing_archetype` asserts a registration inherits its base's radius and `heavy()`; a Drake is 8.0 against the Brute's 6.5 and is heavy) and the 33-registration count. The method's doc now states the exception.
2. **The spawner lives in `director_system`, not a new slot.** A new system with `Commands` would need its own `ApplyDeferred` edge against `miniboss_system` (both draw rng), and the commander's tick order must not depend on a topological tie. Inside the director it also inherits the `peace`/suppress gate.
3. **The lane is drawn in `world.rs`, not through `draw_floor_tell`.** That function takes an `&Enemy`; the lane needs the `Drake` component, and the Sump Leech's alternative (mirror everything into `w4`) would have put the drawn geometry a state behind the hit test. `drake::peck_lane` is still the one geometry for drawing, hit test and bot.
4. **The lane covers the whole Peck state**, not just the out-stroke: the volley re-aims between pecks, and a lane that blinked off for 0.10 s between pecks would hide where the next one goes.
5. **`w4` carries the mirror** (phase, seconds left, aim, warn age, walk) exactly as `floor_sewer` mirrors the leech's tongue, because the bot, `floor_attack_landing` and `damage_mult_from` only ever receive `&Enemy`.
6. **A stunned Drake drops its volley** and walks again (the Sump Leech's rule) — a Sonic Squeak buys the rat out of the three pecks.
7. **The peck volley is iframe-limited**: the rat's 0.9 s window is longer than the 0.22 s between pecks, so a stationary rat takes one peck per volley, not three. That is the same rule every enemy melee hit obeys (`Player::take_damage`), and the contract asked for that path; the ×3 is what makes a *moving* rat's mistake cost one hit rather than three. Flagged as a design observation, not a bug.
8. **`enemy_floors` special-cases the guest** rather than teaching the roster a "guest row": the roster's own doc now says a guest is not a slot and `first_unlock` answers `None`.

## Can the goldens move?

**No.** The determinism goldens (`src/game/ecs/harness.rs`) are six seeded games at 60 s. Everything this change adds is behind `curve_clock(...) >= 150.0` *and* `theme == Sewer`, so no Drake can spawn, no placement rng is drawn and no peck/VFX rng is drawn inside their window; the guest block's only pre-2:30 effect is one compare. I did **not** touch `CASES`, and `drake.rs`'s `no_golden_script_meets_a_drake` asserts the property directly (three floors × 3600 ticks, no Drake, `elapsed < 150`). The map pin (`TRACE_HASHES`) is untouched and cannot move: no levelgen code changed. If a golden *does* move, it is not from this change.

The cost outside the window: a full 15-minute Sewer run gains a Drake roughly every 45 s after 2:30 (and the rng draws of its placement), so `enemy_index_is_fresh_at_every_reader_for_a_full_run`, `full_fifteen_minute_level_per_theme_is_deterministic` and `enemy_cap_holds_through_a_full_run` all see new bodies — they assert properties, not numbers, so they should still pass; the cap assertion has one free slot of headroom (`cap` keeps `ENEMY_CAP - 1` reserved before the boss, the budget tops out at 185).
