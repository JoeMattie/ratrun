## Decisions (made, not re-derived)

- **Owned by `App`, not `World`.** The layer never touches the cached picture, so it
  needs no `PictureKey` entry and no `picture_dirty`: toggling it costs no world
  redraw (pinned by the app test).
- **Colour source is `App::pixels`, not the buffer cells.** `pixel_at(x, 2·row)` /
  `pixel_at(x, 2·row+1)` are the cell's two colours in exact RGB before the terminal
  colour fold, so the guard, the average background and the tint work identically on
  truecolor, 256- and 16-colour terminals (`palette::to_term` folds the result). A
  cell-colour read would have made the layer inert on any non-truecolor terminal.
- **Guard (the mock's):** cell symbol still `▀`; `|top-bot|` summed RGB < `EDGE_GUARD`
  40; no cell within `RAT_CLEAR` 2 of the rat's cell; motes additionally need mean
  luma ≥ `MOTE_LUMA_MIN` 70. bg = mean of the two colours; fg = mean mixed toward the
  particle tint (`lift` %, 100 = the tint itself, which is what sparks use).
- **Hostile pellets rely on the guard.** `World` has no cheap read-only accessor for
  hostile bullet positions (`bullets()` allocates and needs `&mut self`), so I did
  **not** add one: pellets are drawn at full contrast in the hostile layer, so their
  cells fail the edge guard. Damage digits and ink marks are stamped into the pixel
  buffer before the blit and are high-contrast for the same reason.
- **Caps:** 20 motes + 16 sparks + 12 bubbles = `MAX_GLYPHS` 48, halved past 1.5×
  eased zoom. The lattice gate (`3` slots in `128`, 2-cell lattice) gives **21–36
  candidates** on a 120×40 view for real seeds — I replayed the hash in Python, so
  this is measured, not guessed, and it is inside the mock's 20–40 band.
- **Timing:** `step = floor(elapsed × 8)`. Motes drift 1 cell sideways every 12 steps
  (1.5 s) and at most 1 cell up every 32 steps (4 s); glyph morphs every 24 steps.
  Bubbles live `BUBBLE_STEPS` 6 steps (1 cell up per 2 steps, 4 shape stages).
  Sparks live `SPARK_SECS` 0.35 s, 2–3 per fresh number, offsets hashed within ±2 cells,
  moving outward a cell a step and cooling white → gold → ember.
- **Which screens:** `Playing`, `Paused`, `LevelUp`, `Chest`, `Shop`; `Options`/`Controls`/
  `Nest` only when they were opened from `Paused`; **never** `Library` (the mock's
  "never over text") and never a plate that owns the frame. The `--ai` wall builds its
  own buffers in `spectate.rs` and never comes through `draw_game`, so it is excluded
  by construction.
- **Bubble tint by emitter kind, not by theme:** Steam (and a grate mid-plume) → pale
  white `(232,240,246)`; Drip/Fumes → pale teal `(156,232,218)`, both lifted 25 % off
  the cell average. Motes: warm white `(255,236,200)`, 35 %.
- Not built, per the mock: glyph pickups (C) and stippled light-pool edges (E).

## Unverified / likeliest to not compile

Nothing could be compiled here. Ordered by what I would check first:

1. **`stamp`'s cell API.** I used `cell.set_char(ch)` plus direct `cell.fg = ...` /
   `cell.bg = ...` (both patterns already exist in this repo: `framebuffer.rs:408-410`,
   `campaign.rs:848`). `cell.symbol() != "▀"` is the glyph test — the same comparison
   `cursor.rs:541` makes.
2. **`Buffer`/`Cell` equality in the tests.** `glyphfx::tests::a_fixed_frame_is_drawn_identically_twice`
   compares owned `Buffer`s; `app.rs:9475` compares `&Buffer`s, so `PartialEq` + `Debug`
   exist, but the owned-value form is new here.
3. **Field access on `World`.** `w.damage_numbers.items`, `w.level.objects.emitters`,
   `w.level.objects.steam_grates`, `w.level.seed`, `w.zoom_scale`, `w.cam`,
   `w.player.pos`, `w.elapsed` — all read-only and all already used by other render
   modules, but I could not type-check them.
4. **Borrow of `self` in the hook.** `self.glyphfx.draw(frame.buffer_mut(), game, &self.pixels, w)`
   inside `if let Some(w) = self.world.as_ref()` — three disjoint *shared* field borrows;
   if the compiler disagrees, split it into `let pixels = &self.pixels;`.
5. **`app::tests::paused_harness()` assumptions in my app test:** that `world.elapsed`
   is frozen under `Screen::Paused` (yes: `elapsed += dt` only in the tick path,
   `world.rs:1295`), that the game area is ≥ 18 cells wide at 100×40, and that at least
   one particle lands (33 mote candidates + 3 sparks around a fresh number 18 cells
   right of the rat, on flat floor). If that last assert ever trips it means the guard
   is rejecting everything, not that the wiring is wrong — the message says so.
6. `MOTE_ONE_IN`/`MOTE_GATE` are `u64`; `MOTE_LATTICE` is `i32` and is cast to `u64`
   once. If a lint objects to the `as` casts, `u64::from`/`try_from` are fine.

picture including unlit-but-lit-by-art areas, and its own "lit pool" test is the simple
