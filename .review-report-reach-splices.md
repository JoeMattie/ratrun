## 1. Whip and laser reach capped to the unzoomed screen

The "screen" is the `Viewport` resource (zoom 1.0, centred on the rat) — never the render zoom.

- `src/game/ecs/systems/approach.rs:70` — `in_sim_view_inset(pos, center, viewport, inset)`:
  the rectangle `in_sim_view` already tests, with a margin (clamped so it cannot invert).
- `src/game/ecs/systems/approach.rs:86` — `sim_view_ray_exit(from, dir, center, viewport, inset)`
  (`f32`): slab ray/rect intersection, the distance at which a beam leaves that rectangle.
  Both helpers sit next to `in_sim_view` (which spawn.rs's approach boost uses) and are
  `pub`, so a third weapon can use them.
- **Spiked Tail** (`src/game/ecs/systems/weapon_spiketail.rs`):
  - `:113` `const LASH_VIEW_INSET: f32 = 4.0`.
  - `:274` the enemy pick and `:302` the boss hit-shape pick in `pick_lash_target` now also
    require `in_sim_view_inset(.., from, view, LASH_VIEW_INSET)`; `view` is a new last
    parameter of `pick_lash_target`/`fire_spiked_tail` (`:241`, `:351`).
  - `weapons.rs`: `fire_weapons_system` takes `Res<Viewport>` (`:166`) and `fire_one` gained a
    trailing `view: Vec2` (`:1552`), threaded to `fire_spiked_tail` (`:2150`).
  - Barbed Wire goes through the same `pick_lash_target`, so it inherits the rule (its
    farthest-hookable long cast can no longer pick an off-screen body).
- **Laser Eyes** (`src/game/ecs/systems/weapons_wave3b.rs`):
  - `:227` in `sweep_step`, `len = (nominal * wall_stop).min(sim_view_ray_exit(origin, dir, origin, view, 0.0))`
    — the wall stop is kept and the screen stop is taken as the min.
  - `laser_eyes_system` takes `Res<Viewport>` (`:350`) and passes `viewport.0` to all four
    `sweep_step` calls (`:506`, `:527`, `:576`, `:595`).
  - The hit test and `LaserFx::beam`/`beam_b` are computed from the same `len`, so the drawn
    beam (art_rat / render/lighting/sources.rs read `LaserFx`) is exactly what hits.

Decisions (mine, permitted):
- The laser's rectangle is centred on **the cast's own origin** (`fx.origin`, `copy.origin`),
  not on the rat's current position. Reason: the beam is drawn from that origin and the owner
  asked that Carbon Copy "uses the lengths of the sweep it copies"; centring on the origin makes
  a replay reproduce the original lengths exactly, whatever the rat has done since. The live
  beam is anchored where the rat was when it fired (≤ 0.34 s of drift).
- The 4 px inset is applied to the lash only (its burst centre must be clearly on screen); the
  beam is cut exactly at the rectangle's edge, which is what the player can see.
- The boss's *hit points* are subject to the same test as enemy bodies — a lash whose impact
  point is off screen would draw its burst off screen.
- The 0.12 s wind-up still follows its body, so the impact point can drift a few px from where
  it was picked; documented on `LASH_VIEW_INSET`. Freezing it would have been a second
  behaviour change.
