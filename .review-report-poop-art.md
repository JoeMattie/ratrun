## Design decisions

- **No cross-file constants dependency**: `ARM_AGE` (0.25) and `DRYING_AGE` (12.0) are module-local constants matching the shared contract rather than importing `weapon::DROPPING_ARM`/`DROPPING_LIFE`, so this module compiles regardless of whether the code worker makes those constants `pub`.
- **Two-channel shimmer without hue cancellation**: Early prototype mixed violet `(206, 106, 190)` and green `(150, 224, 128)` on one step, which desaturated toward grey on pale tiles. Kept each drifting pixel pure violet or pure pale green instead.
- **Tight lower-right keyline (no diagonal corner)**: Dropping the `(dx + 1, dy + 1)` corner kept 4-pellet clusters (`seed 2`, `seed 6`) airy and unmistakably smaller than a coin while still popping off Sewer, Kitchen, Lab, Laundry, Printworks, and Flatbed (checked at 1.0× albedo and 0.60× ambient lighting).
- **Drying step**: Begins immediately at `age > 12.0` (`k = 0.58..1.0` across `12.0..14.0`) so any `age > 12.0` is immediately distinguishable from an armed dropping and dries further until despawn.
- **No module registration touched**: Per file-scope rules, `src/game/mod.rs` was not edited; the coordinator / code worker wires `pub mod art_droppings;` and the `World::draw_scene` call site.
