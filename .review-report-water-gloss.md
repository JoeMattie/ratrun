# Water gloss: puddles that glint and reflect

**Nothing here was compiled or run** (worker rules). Everything below is written, and the tests are the ones I would have run.

## What was built

- **`src/render/lighting/water.rs`** (new; declared in `src/render/lighting.rs:31`). A lit-space pixel stage over `MaterialKind::Water` pixels, called from `engine.rs:1068-1084` right after `softshadow::reflections_with` and before `air::composite`. It runs three passes, in this order:
  - **C. Body mirrors** (`body_mirrors`, Full only). One pass over the buffer with a per-column "last creature row" array on the stack (2048 columns). A water pixel `d` px below the nearest creature pixel above it (1 ≤ d < `MIRROR_REACH`) shows the creature pixel at `(x + wobble, y − 2d + 1)`. That pixel must itself be creature-tagged. The image is darkened 30 %, tinted 15 % toward the water and blended at `BODY_MIRROR × (REACH − d)/(REACH − 1)`. Creature pixels and non-water pixels are never written, and no body in reach means no write.
  - **B. Lamp mirrors** (`lamp_mirrors`, Full and Hard). Each `LightTag::Fixture` in `frame.lights` (at most 5, steady gain ≥ 48) becomes a soft ellipse centred `MIRROR_K × height` below the lamp. Its core is pulled 60 % toward white, and each row is shifted ±1 px by a hash of the world row and the 3 Hz step. It only brightens, is clipped to water on floor samples, and is gated by the sample's received light (`spec`).
  - **A. Glints** (`glints` / `glint_at`, Full and Hard).
    - A water pixel becomes a candidate when `hash(world x, world y, step3) & 255 < GLINT_DENSITY`, or `< RIPPLE_DENSITY` on a live ripple ring.
    - The normal is octave 1 (value noise on 8 px cells, tilt 40) plus octave 2 (per-pixel grain, tilt 22) plus an outward tilt on a ripple ring.
    - The lobe is `cos^48` above a 0.30 floor, rescaled. It is evaluated against the sample's dominant light (`LightInfo`). On Full it is also evaluated against the rat's aura, computed directly from `frame.rat`: radius 66 (58 on the Lab), height 12, a one-tap wall guard at the midpoint. The brighter of the two lobes is used.
    - Full leans the view vector by `(view centre − pixel) × GLINT_VIEW_K`. Hard has no view term and no rat lobe.
    - Brightness is a lift toward white, scaled by the received light (no glint under `GLINT_MIN_LIGHT`). The colour is pulled `GLINT_WHITE`/256 toward white.
    - A hash bit turns 3 in 16 glints into a 2 px dash at 60 %.
    - Sparsity is guaranteed by construction: at most 7.8 % candidates, plus their dashes, gives ≤ 9.3 % of pixels before the lobe cuts most of them.
  - **Ripples.** The ripples come from the cosmetic `PuddleSteps` resource via `water::gather_ripples` (engine passes `scene.ecs`). The radii come from the new `art_puddle::ripple_radii`, so the glints ride the exact ring `draw_ripple` draws.
- **Hooks.**
  - `specular.rs:461-497,579`: `apply` skips non-creature Water pixels when `water::owns_water(mode)`. Its flat per-sample sheen was the "flat teal patch". The test-only `run` keeps the old behaviour.
  - `softshadow.rs:721-772,841`: `reflections_with(…, skip_water)` drops the wet-brick streak on water. `reflections` is now `#[cfg(test)]`, so the existing tests are unchanged.
  - `fx.rs`: `strength::WATER = 1.0`.
- **The switch.**
  - `water::owns_water(mode)` is true when `WATER > 0`, the tier is Full or Hard, and `RATRUN_FX_SKIP` does not list `water`. Coarse, Compact and Ansi16 draw nothing.
  - With fx off the stage never runs and the art is unchanged, so the frame is byte-identical.
  - With `WATER = 0`, specular and reflections light the water exactly as before, the art is the old look, and `run` returns before writing.
- **D. Art** (`art_puddle.rs`). `draw_puddle_among_with(…, glossy)`; `draw_puddle_among` is the `glossy = false` wrapper, byte-identical to before.
  - World passes `glossy = pb.materials_on() && water_gloss_enabled()` (`world.rs:3596`). Materials are on exactly when fx runs at Full or Hard, the tiers the water stage runs on.
  - Glossy deepens the body by `GLOSS_DEEPEN` 12 % × depth.
  - **Removed in glossy:** the 4 Hz held-step shimmer glints (2 px dashes), and the still 3 px "wavelet" dashes (painted highlights).
  - **Kept:** the waterline rim, the pale meniscus (it is the shore, not a sparkle) and the floating Sewer specks and Laundry suds.
  - `draw_ripple` now gets its radii from `ripple_radii_at`, with the same expressions, so it is byte-identical.
- **G.** CLAUDE.md: the lighting-effects paragraph and the Standing water bullet.

## Expected cost (estimated, not measured)

- C is one byte read per buffer pixel plus a hash per row. At 120×80 that is about 10k reads, and only water pixels within 10 px under a body do a blend. Expect about 0.02–0.04 ms. The per-column bitset is built in, so there is no naive upward scan.
- A does one mask read per pixel and one hash per water pixel. Only about 8 % of water pixels (the candidates) do value noise, two normalisations and one `powi`. Expect about 0.03–0.06 ms.
- B is ≤ 5 ellipses of ~100 px each, under 0.01 ms.
- **Total: about 0.05–0.1 ms a frame at 120×80.**
- Bytes: glints change ≤ ~5 % of puddle pixels per 3 Hz step. That is less churn than the 4 Hz painted shimmer it replaces, which was 0.6–0.9 % of pixels per step but on every step boundary. Lamp mirrors are static. Body mirrors move with the bodies.

## Decisions

- The grid keeps only the dominant light, so the "second lobe" on Full is the rat's aura, evaluated directly. Taking the max of the two lobes avoids double-counting when the aura *is* the dominant light. Hard uses only the dominant lobe.
- The rat lobe has no shadow bake, only a one-tap midpoint wall guard.
- Ripple positions *are* cheaply available (`PuddleSteps` via `scene.ecs`), so ring tilt and density are implemented.
- The lamp mirror and the 3 Hz step read the light's **steady** gain, so a flicker does not repaint the image. Glint brightness still follows `info.dom`, which carries the sconce's 3 Hz held flicker.
- **Acid Spit pools also use `pool_pen` (Water), so they glint and mirror too.** I judged that right for a liquid. If not, give pools their own pen.
- Hard runs B and the cheaper A, but not C, per the brief.
- The view-term sign is `(view centre − pixel)`, the physical view vector toward an eye above the centre. The brief's `(pixel − centre)` is the same with a negative K.
